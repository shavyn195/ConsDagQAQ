--LIMITATIONS--
1101:Limitations
1102:Constraints are built once with a strong proprietary
1103:model (GPT-5.5) and refined on execution traces
1104:of training tasks, which requires access to such
1105:traces and incurs a one-time cost. Our constraints
1106:check each tool call against the call history, so they
1107:detect errors that are visible in that history: wrong
1108:or miscopied arguments, calls in the wrong state,
1109:missing prerequisites. They cannot detect failures
1110:that depend on hidden environment state (e.g., a
1111:credit limit the agent has not queried), and they
1112:cannot detect omissions, where the agent never
1113:makes a necessary call. This is why recall is much
1114:lower than precision (Table 1), and why constraints
1115:help little for agents whose failures are mostly of
1116:these kinds, such as Qwen3.5-9B.
1117:Improvements in task success are largest when
1118:tool errors are costly or invisible and when the
1119:agent makes many call-level errors. In the standard
1120:AppWorld setting, success gains are small, and a
1121:strong model that rarely makes these errors (Claude
1122:Sonnet-5) benefits little. Warnings can also mis-
1123:lead: a false alarm may cause an agent to retry a
1124:call that was valid.
